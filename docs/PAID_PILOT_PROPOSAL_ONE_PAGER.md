# Paid VERTEX Decision Quality Pilot

## Target buyer

Incubator directors, university entrepreneurship center directors, accelerator program managers, and economic-development program leads who run recurring founder cohorts.

Best-fit first buyer:

A Program Director or Head of Entrepreneurship running recurring cohorts of roughly 10-50 teams, supported by multiple facilitators or mentors, who must demonstrate participant development or program outcomes to a dean, sponsor, public funder, foundation, board, or senior leadership team.

## Pilot offer

VERTEX gives the institution a structured Decision Quality Pilot for early-stage founders. The pilot captures baseline intuition, guides teams through VERTEX, supports facilitator intervention, and produces Decision Memos plus a Cohort Outcome Report.

VERTEX does not predict startup success or guarantee validation. It shows what changed between initial intuition and final decision.

## Structure

Recommended pilot:

- 4-6 weeks
- 10-20 founder teams
- 1 facilitator or program lead
- 1 buyer debrief at the end
- 1 final decision on whether to repeat, expand, or stop

Flow:

1. Buyer kickoff
2. Founder onboarding
3. Baseline capture
4. Golden Path workflow
5. Facilitator review and comments
6. Baseline/post rubric scoring
7. Decision Memo review
8. Cohort Outcome Report
9. Buyer debrief and next-cohort decision

## Included deliverables

- VERTEX platform access for the pilot cohort
- Decision-readiness methodology
- Facilitator review cadence
- Intervention Radar for weekly facilitator prioritization
- Baseline/post measurement
- Decision Memos for completed cases
- Cohort Outcome Report
- Buyer debrief
- Follow-up recommendation for renewal or next cohort

## Pricing hypothesis

These are pilot pricing hypotheses, not validated market prices.

| Offer | Range | Use when |
|---|---:|---|
| Design-partner pilot | USD 2,500-4,000 | Buyer gives deep feedback and case-study access |
| Standard paid pilot | USD 4,000-7,500 | 10-20 teams, normal facilitator support |
| High-touch pilot | USD 7,500-12,000 | Custom workshop, deeper debrief, more facilitator involvement |

Current recommended commercial test:

`EUR 5,500` for a 4-6 week Decision Quality Pilot with 10-20 teams.

The commercial experiment is whether a buyer will approve this as a real paid pilot, not whether they say the price sounds reasonable.

## Success measures

Feasibility / product behavior:

- More than 80% of teams complete the core workflow or have a clearly documented intervention reason.
- Baselines are captured before substantive program intervention.
- DecisionRecords and Decision Memos are completed for a useful share of cases.
- Facilitators use the Intervention Radar during delivery.

Proximal founder-reasoning change:

- Teams discover new stakeholders, risks, or economic constraints.
- Important assumptions change or become more explicit.
- Decisions or next experiments become more specific.
- Before/post rubric scores create a structured change signal.

Institutional utility:

- Facilitators judge a meaningful share of Intervention Radar alerts useful.
- Substantive interventions are resolved or carried into the debrief.
- The buyer uses or shares the Cohort Outcome Report.
- The buyer wants another cohort, renewal, referral, or paid expansion.

Do not claim causal proof from a simple before/after pilot. The pilot can show observed change and buyer utility; it cannot isolate VERTEX from mentors, workshops, customers, time, or the rest of the program.

## What VERTEX is not

- not a CRM
- not an LMS
- not mentor matching
- not application management
- not investor deal flow
- not a startup prediction engine
- not a guarantee of validation

## Buyer decision ask

Approve one paid 4-6 week Decision Quality Pilot with a defined founder cohort, facilitator owner, and debrief date.

Buyer discovery questions:

- Which upcoming cohort is the most realistic place to test this?
- Who else must approve a EUR 5,500 pilot?
- What would the report need to show for you to fund the next cohort?
- What would stop you from running this with that cohort?
