What is ready for the first delivery?

The first version does not need every adapter fully automated. It needs one complete, believable path that teaches the method and produces contract-backed artifacts.

  • Alex and SynapMap remain the human-readable front of problem and system work.
  • D-Predict is represented as a bounded adoption/resistance hypothesis in the golden case.
  • Billie is the live financial layer for price, margin and projection.
  • DecisionRecord is the final traceable output.
