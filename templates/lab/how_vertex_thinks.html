Theory into practice

VERTEX teaches founders to read the whole system.

The method combines Design Thinking, Systems Thinking, QBI, behavioral decision science and financial intelligence into one practical path: open the idea, map the system, read behavior, read the numbers and make a traceable decision.

Design Thinking

Open the problem.

VERTEX starts by keeping the question alive long enough to avoid premature solutioning.

  • Empathy
  • Reframing
  • Assumption testing

Systems Thinking

See what the idea touches.

A decision changes actors, dependencies, incentives and constraints around the founder.

  • Stakeholders
  • Relationships
  • Second-order effects

QBI

Understand uncertainty before collapse.

QBI is the deeper theory behind framings, behavioral correlation, contextual loss and commitment.

  • Multiple readings can coexist
  • Actors influence each other
  • Context can destroy coherence

Billie principle

The numbers are not there to punish the idea.

They are there to tell the founder what the idea needs in order to survive. Billie makes pricing, margin, break-even and cash flow visible before the team commits to a bigger bet.
